## Vellmark: the Showcase

**An open-world RPG where you rewrite reality with a word.**

Throw open bronze doors that have been locked for forty years. Mend a broken bridge
under your own feet, set a blade alight, calm what guards an orchard, and fight with a
weapon in one hand and a word in the other.

Wake in an open grave on a hill above a small village, with a gravedigger looking down
at you and no name anyone can write. Climb to the stone at the top, take your first word
from it, and go down into the valley to find out what else will listen.

And then come close to anything. The walls, the grass, the bark, the bridge, the blade
in your hand: every surface is written, in print too fine to see until you are near
enough to read it, and what is written is what it is. Change the word, and the world
changes with it.

### What is in the Showcase

- One valley, at dusk and dawn and night: a village, a graveyard hill, a river with a
  broken bridge, an orchard on the far bank, and a short dungeon under the hill.
- Seven words of magic, each with its own sound, each changing things for real: doors,
  fires, lamps, the bridge, creatures, your own weapon.
- Fights with a blade and a word together: block, stagger, set your weapon burning,
  freeze what splits.
- Loot worth stopping for, a merchant, and a character to dress.
- Wick, the Oakes, and the village of Little Penance, with their own words to say.
- Something black on the horizon, which crosses out what it touches.
- Photo mode, with a split frame that shows the world as you see it on one side and as
  it is written on the other.

**About 30 to 45 minutes.** An early, unfinished slice of a larger game: one valley,
built to the quality the whole game will have. It collects nothing about you.

**Platforms:** Windows, Linux, and macOS (macOS when it can be signed).
**Controls:** keyboard and mouse, or a controller.

### Accessibility

Remappable controls, letter size and interface size, high contrast, colour-blind
colours, captions for the sounds that matter, reduced motion, fewer flashes, and
creatures' states said in words.

### Made with

Its own engine, written for this game. Its music and sounds are made as it plays. The
use of AI in making it is set out in full on the AI disclosure, which travels with the
game.
