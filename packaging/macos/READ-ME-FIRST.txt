Vellmark: a development build for the owner's Mac.

To play
  Right-click Vellmark.app and choose Open. macOS asks once, because this build was
  downloaded and is not signed. Choose Open again.

To start in the valley instead of the yard
  Right-click valley.command and choose Open.

To run the performance check
  Right-click perf-run.command and choose Open. It runs for about a minute and puts
  vellmark-perf-report.txt on your Desktop. Send that file back.

Keys in this build
  W A S D and the mouse    move and look
  Escape                   free the mouse; again to leave
  F1                       the profiler view
  F2                       the free camera (Space up, Left Ctrl down, Left Shift fast)
  F3                       switch the glyph layer off and on
  F4                       the tuning panel
  F5                       light the lantern or put it out
  F6                       step through the close-up looks (living is the default)
  F7                       break the nearest breakable thing into its letters
  `                        the console; type help. Try: heat door 0.9, break door 0.1

What to check is listed in docs/PLAYTEST_CHECKLIST.md in the repository.
