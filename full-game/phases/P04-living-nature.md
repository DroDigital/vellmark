# Phase 04: Living nature

**Goal:** The Vale feels alive: vegetation, water, skies, weather, and particles, all made of letters.

**Suggested model:** Opus 5.5, high effort. Rendering work that follows patterns set in Phase 03.

**Hand to Haiku 5.5:** Plant and prop placement data, wind and weather parameter tables, and asset import records. Rules are in `CLAUDE.md` under Models and helpers.

**Release:** Release 1

**Requires:** Phase 03 finished and tagged `phase-03-done`.

## Scope

Grass, trees with levels of detail, water, clouds, weather, particles, aurora, and the Erasure as seen from a distance. Snow, embers, marsh fog, and the Verso's look are built with their own Quires.
