# History and hidden truth

## The public story

In the beginning there was the Page, and the Page was blank, and this was considered untidy.

Six Authors took up pens and wrote the land of Vellmark line by line. They wrote rivers before they wrote thirst, which the Compositor still regards as good planning. All that they wrote was gathered into the **Great Ledger**, where every person, stone, river, and goat has an entry, and where it is recorded what each is owed and what each will owe.

To be entered is to be real. Priests read births into the Ledger's lesser copies. Courts settle disputes by consulting it. Fate is simply the part of your entry you have not reached yet, and most people prefer not to look.

## The six Authors

| Author | Domain | Sign | Temperament |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Rubricator** | Judgment, fire, blood-debt | Red ink | Exact and never forgiving |
| **Mother Vellum** | Bodies, beasts, birth, hunger | Bare hide | Kind the way weather is kind |
| **The Compositor** | Stone, roads, walls, order | A set line of type | Fussy and proud |
| **Gall** | Grief, memory, the dead | Black oak-gall ink | Patient and sour |
| **The Gilder** | Gold, trade, vanity, light | A gilded capital | Charming and untrustworthy |
| **Saint Slip** | Error, luck, drunks, thieves | A blot | The youngest, and always sorry |

## The hidden truth

**The Great Ledger is full.**

It reached its last leaf more than a thousand Lines ago. Since then, it has made room by scraping the oldest entries and reusing the vellum. A palimpsest is a page used twice, and the world has been one for a very long time. This is why the dead are forgotten within a few generations. Grief fades because the paper is needed.

By Line 4,090, there were no dead left old enough to scrape. The Ledger began taking the living, then places, then whole valleys. The Erasure is its housekeeping, carried out by its proofing faculty, **the Corrector**, and its winged instruments, the **Redactors**.

### The Authors

The Authors were never gods. They were the first six clerks, ordinary people who found the Page and wrote themselves into it as divine. Their entries are the only ones in the Ledger that cannot be scraped, which is the true cause of the shortage. Six immortal entries, each annotated for four thousand years, take up an astonishing amount of room.

### The Last Clerk

The Last Scriptorium stands in the far north, around the Ledger itself. Its keepers were the only people who knew the truth.

In Line 4,111, the Corrector issued an instruction. To continue the Ledger, a final line was to be entered: *Let all prior entries be scraped, and the work begin again.* The whole world would be blank, and the Page would be clean for a new Writing.

That line needed a living hand. The duty fell to the youngest clerk, the **Clerk of the Last Page**: the player.

The Clerk took a scraper and struck out their own entry instead. Name, history, and memory went with it. There was now nobody qualified to write the line, and one person alive outside the Ledger, able to choose. The act should have unmade the Clerk. It did not, because the Clerk used **the Penknife of Saint Slip**, the one tool in creation made by a mistake.

Their teacher, **Odile Marsh**, Master of the Scriptorium, fled south with the secret. The Corrector cannot write the final line without a hand, so it is doing the job the slow way, one village at a time.

### Why only you remember

When something is erased, its entry is scraped, along with everything that refers to it in anyone else's entry. Memories are entries. You have no entry to edit, so your memories cannot be corrected.

### The three possible futures

| Choice | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Rebind** | Scrape enough of the "less important" to free up pages, and restore the Ledger's order |
| **Blank** | Let the final line be written and the world end kindly and completely |
| **Free** | Destroy the Ledger, ending fate, the Erasure, and in time, all magic |
| **Colophon** (secret) | Write your own name and become the next Author |
