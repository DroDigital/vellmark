### Loose words (12)

Some words came off their stones long ago and have lain ever since on small things, doing a little of their work. A loose word is learned by reading the thing it is written on. Each Quire holds two, in waiting places that the Quire's own words open, as set out in `docs/brief/4c-content-rules.md`.

| Word | Class | Written on | Quire | Typical use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SHARP | Integrity | A miller's whetstone | Scrivenhold Vale | Blades bite deeper; thorns, edges, and tongues cut |
| HONEST | Mind | A pair of market scales | Scrivenhold Vale | A person tells the truth; a creature shows every blow before it lands |
| QUIET | Sound | The clapper of a bell that does not ring | The Foxing Wood | Silence footsteps, alarms, and casters |
| LOST | Concealment | A signpost with no arms | The Foxing Wood | A creature cannot find its way, or you |
| LOUD | Sound | A ship's bell | Saltmargin | Draw everything in earshot to one place |
| LARGE | Size | A fisherman's account of his catch | Saltmargin | Grow a creature or a thing: more reach, more weight, an easier target |
| SLOW | Motion | A sundial under moss | The Blotmere | Drag a creature, a trap, or a falling thing to a crawl |
| SMALL | Size | A thimble | The Blotmere | Shrink a creature or a thing: through gaps, under doors, into a pocket |
| FAST | Motion | A courier's boot | The Cinder Folio | Quicken a blade, a mill wheel, a fuse, or yourself |
| BRAVE | Mind | A soldier's medal, never collected | The Cinder Folio | Allies hold their ground; enemies refuse to retreat |
| STILL | Motion | A weathervane frozen in place | The Whitemarch | Stop a thing where it is: an arrow in the air, a swinging blade |
| BLUNT | Integrity | A butter knife | The Whitemarch | Take the edge off a weapon, a claw, or a remark |

**Size has limits.** `LARGE` and `SMALL` change a thing by about half again or half, with its reach and its real mass. Indoors, `LARGE` is refused where there is no room: "There is no room for that here." Bosses and people the story needs refuse both.

**Refusal lines.** Each word and target class without an effect has a dry response, for example: "The stone declines." "That is already as open as it intends to be." "Not without a signature."

## Typos

Each word has at most one typo. Rules are in `docs/brief/5h-signature-mechanics.md`. Typos are brief, never cruel, and never useless.

| Word | Typo | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| OPEN | OVEN | The target grows hot inside. A chest bakes whatever it holds |
| LOCKED | LICKED | The target is damp and mortified. A creature loses its next action |
| LIT | LIP | The target answers back, reading one line of its own text aloud, loudly |
| DARK | BARK | The target barks once. Every Blot Hound in earshot comes to look |
| COLD | GOLD | For a moment the target is worth a great deal. Bandits and merchants cannot look away. The value fades with the word |
| WARM | WORM | The target wriggles. Small things crawl a short way, and a creature loses its footing |
| BURNING | TURNING | The target turns to face the other way. Doors swing, and enemies show their backs |
| WET | NET | The target is tangled where it stands |
| DRY | CRY | The target weeps, and is `WET` afterwards. A creature stops to grieve |
| LIGHT | NIGHT | Darkness pools around the target, and nearby lights go `DARK` |
| HEAVY | HEAVE | The target is thrown a short way in the direction the writer faces |
| CALM | CLAM | The target shuts. It cannot speak, call for help, or cast |
| ASLEEP | A SHEEP | The target believes it. It grazes, and will not fight unless struck |
| HOSTILE | HOSTEL | The target lets anyone in. A creature stops defending its ground |
| WHOLE | HOLE | A gap opens in the target: a weak point in armour, a way through a fence |
| BROKEN | BROKER | The target wants to make a deal. A creature drops something to be left alone |

| ABSENT | ASSENT | The target agrees with the last thing said. In a fight, it nods and hesitates |
| LOYAL | ROYAL | The target believes it is in charge. Its own kind obey it or resent it |
| SHARP | SHARK | The target bites whatever touches it next |
| BLUNT | BRUNT | The target takes the next blow meant for anyone near it |
| QUIET | QUILT | The target is padded. Falls and blunt blows do little to it, or by it |
| LOUD | CLOUD | A small cloud gathers over the target and rains. It is `WET` afterwards |
| FAST | FEAST | The target becomes food, or sits down to some. Beasts come for it |

AFRAID, NAMELESS, and HONEST have no typo. An honest mistake is still honest, and so nothing happens. A smudged AFRAID wastes its Ink. NAMELESS has never once gone wrong, and nobody finds that reassuring.

## Sorts

Four punctuation sorts change how a word is delivered: `!` Exclaim, `,` Comma, `...` Ellipsis, and `?` Query. Each is a single worn piece of type, found once. Rules are in `docs/brief/5h-signature-mechanics.md`.

## Rarity

Six tiers, told apart by ink treatment and a tier mark and never by colour alone: Plain, Fair, Endorsed, Engrossed, Illuminated, and Canonical. A scrivener would know them at once. A Plain thing is merely made. A Fair one was copied out properly by someone who signed it. An Endorsed one carries a clause, an Engrossed one is a finished deed, and an Illuminated one took a year of somebody's eyesight. What each tier carries is defined in `docs/brief/5d-loot.md`.

## Weapon types (8)

Sword, Axe, Mace, Dagger, Greatsword, Staff, Bow, and Shield. They share four movesets: One-handed (Sword, Axe, Mace, Dagger), Two-handed (Greatsword, Staff), Bow, and Shield. Each type has one trait, listed in the brief. A Staff holds one word of Emendation.

Most people in Vellmark who fight carry steel in one hand and keep the other free. They do not know why. The Unentered does.

## Materials

Iron, Marcher Steel, Saltwork, Rootwood, Slagglass, Blacksteel, Bookplate (light vellum armour), and Gilt. Each has one trait, listed in the brief.

## Item naming and clauses

Prefixes come from makers and materials. A maker's name stands for a bonus, and there are 20 makers, written in full during Phase 07. Suffixes come from legal language, and each suffix is a clause, this world's enchantment, with one effect. There are 30 clauses, also written in Phase 07. An Illuminated item also carries one of 12 word clauses, which change what a single word does, set after a comma.

Examples: "Tally's Iron Mace" (Fair), "Long Bow in Perpetuity" (Endorsed), "Tally's Iron Mace of Prior Claim" (Engrossed), "Tally's Gilt Sword of Reasonable Doubt, Without Prejudice" (Illuminated).
