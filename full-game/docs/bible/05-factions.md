## The Ledger Guard

**Warriors. Motto: "Written and witnessed."**
**Leader:** Lord Registrar Corvin Thale. **Field commander:** Marshal Hester Quillon. **Base:** The Upper Hand, Scrivenhold.

They uphold the Ledger, collect tithes, and keep the roads. Most are decent people who believe a world with records is safer than one without. Their ranks are Tallyman, Auditor, Sergeant-at-Ink, and Marshal. Their arc moves from defending order to discovering what the order requires.

**Rewards:** heavy armour, Guard writ for free passage, and the Knight's Tally set.

## The Scriveners' College

**Mages. Motto: "Check it again."**
**Leader:** Provost Emeric Sable. **Key member:** Odile Marsh, Reader Emerita. **Base:** College Row, Scrivenhold.

Scholars of Emendation, who can only perform weak local corrections without a Standing Page, and resent you very much. Their ranks are Reader, Corrector (lowercase, and unrelated, which they are tired of explaining), Fellow, and Provost. Their arc asks what scholars do when the answer is unbearable.

**Rewards:** Ink capacity, library access, and the Reader's Vestments set.

## The Margin Folk

**Thieves and outlaws. Motto: "Nobody reads the small print."**
**Leader:** Inter Pell, called Mistress Interlinear. **Base:** The Gutters, Scrivenhold, with cells in Deckle Quay and Stilt Parish.

They live by forging entries, erasing debts, and moving goods along the world's blank edges. They call the rooftops of Scrivenhold "the margin," and have rules against harming anyone who can't afford it. Their arc ends with the greatest forgery in history, and a question of whether a lie can save people.

**Rewards:** lockpicks, forged writs, a rooftop network, and the Marginalia Leathers set.

## The Blank Choir

**Cult of the Erasure. Motto: "Let it be clean."**
**Leader:** The Cantor, Ysolde Wan. **Second:** Precentor Aldhelm Sour. **Base:** The Choirhouse, the Blotmere.

Quiet, kind, and terrifying. They nurse the dying, comfort the bereaved, and teach that oblivion is the only debt forgiven in full. Many joined after reading their own fates. Ysolde's daughter was written to die at nine, and did. Members can join and rise without ever harming anyone, until the final quests.

**Rewards:** Silence abilities, pale robes, and the Choir Whites set.

## The Errata

**Monster hunters. Motto: "We regret the error."**
**Leader:** Hob Corrigan. **Base:** The Errata Lodge in Tallymarket.

Professional hunters of things that should not exist: misprinted beasts, rogue marginalia, and anything the Ledger has listed twice. They invoice for everything. Their arc shifts when a contract turns out to be for something that has every right to exist.

**Rewards:** species-specific gear, a working bestiary, and the Hunter's Corrigenda set.
