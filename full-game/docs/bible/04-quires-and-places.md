# Quires and places

Totals in the canon (build tiers are in `docs/SCOPE.md`): 1 capital, 5 Quire seats, 8 towns and villages, 20 small sites, 40 dungeons (12 Hero, 28 kit-built), and 120 points of interest. `[H]` marks a Hero dungeon. Kits are Crypt, Mine, Fort, Cave, Ruin, and Tower. `[Page: WORD]` marks a Standing Page.

## Scrivenhold Vale

A broad river basin of barley, mills, and litigation, where three old roads meet. The River Quill runs through it. Palette: warm parchment, oak-brown ink, slate-blue shadow, and a brick-red accent. Sheriff: none; the Vale answers directly to the Lord Registrar.

**Capital: Scrivenhold.** A walled city on a stepped hill.

| District | Character |
|---|---|
| The Upper Hand | Castle, Hall of Record, and the Lord Registrar's offices |
| College Row | The Scriveners' College and its Index Stacks |
| Tallymarket | Merchants, inns, smiths |
| The Gutters | Poor lower district, canals, and Margin Folk rooftops |
| Reading-Out | Temple quarter and great cemetery |

**Towns and villages:** Ambry (market town), Little Penance (village, erased in Q3).

**Small sites:** Wick's Rest (gravedigger's cottage), Tollow Mill, The Second Helping (inn), Oake Farm, Barley Reckoning (farm), Ferry-at-Quill.

## The Erasure

Nearly pure white paper with characters thinning out. There is little sound. It grows in three stages, after Q7, Q14, and Q17. The remaining inhabitants are losing their words.

**Village:** Last Ditto, a settlement whose people repeat what they can still remember.

## The Verso

The reverse side of the page, about 2 square kilometres. White ink on black paper, with all text mirrored. Entered through Torn Pages in Q8, Q14, and Q19, and by optional secrets.

## Travel notes

- The Compositor's Roads connect Scrivenhold to each seat.
- Road signs are written in full sentences and frequently pass judgment.
- Fast travel is by waystones called **Bookmarks**, activated on first visit.
