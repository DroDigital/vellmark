## The game in one paragraph

Vellmark was written into being, and everything in it has an entry in the Great Ledger. Now a silent white blankness is scraping the world back to bare vellum, and when something is erased, everyone forgets it ever was. Only you remember, because you have no entry. You are the Unentered: unrecorded, unbilled, and unexplainable. You can read the world, rewrite it, and carry the memory of everything it loses.

## The five truths

Do not reveal these before the named quest.

1. **The Erasure is housekeeping.** The Great Ledger is full. It is scraping old entries so that new ones can be written. (Q12)
2. **Forgetting has always been how the world made room.** The dead fade from memory because their entries are being reused. (Q12)
3. **The Authors were people.** They were the first clerks, and they wrote themselves in as gods. (Q17, and the secret chains)
4. **You struck yourself out.** You were the Clerk of the Last Page. You were ordered to write the line that would scrape the whole world, and you removed the only hand that could write it. (Q14)
5. **Odile always knew.** She was your teacher, and she could not tell you, because anything written or spoken as your history could let the Ledger find you. (Q13 and Q14)

## Themes

- **Fate or freedom:** is a guaranteed life worth more than an uncertain one?
- **Memory as love:** being remembered is the only afterlife anyone can prove.
- **The mercy and cruelty of forgetting:** the Blank Choir is not stupid.
- **The bureaucracy of the sacred:** the gods keep accounts, and their paperwork is terrifying.

## Tone

Melancholy first, funny second, and never ironic about grief. Picture a folk ballad written by a tired county clerk. People here have made small, stubborn accommodations with the end of the world. A widow files a complaint about it. A goat herder charges the apocalypse for damages.

## Canon rules

- The Erasure removes things from memory for everyone entered in the Ledger. Written records of the erased turn blank. Physical traces survive only if they were made by, or are carried by, the Unentered.
- The Verso holds the reversed impression of everything ever written on the page's other side, so erased things can leave a mirror image there.
- No one can write the player's name. Ink slides off. NPCs call the player "you there," "stranger," or "Unentered." In co-op, the second player is **the Witness**, the co-signatory every entry once required.
- Emendation changes what an Inscription says. It does not create anything from nothing.
- The Authors never speak directly, except through the Author quests' written marginalia.
- There are no elves, dwarves, dragons, or orcs. Nothing is drawn from any existing fantasy property.
