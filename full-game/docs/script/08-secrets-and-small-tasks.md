# Script: secrets and small tasks

## Secret questlines (8)

Found only by reading the world closely. None appears in the journal until its first clue is read.

| # | Chain | First clue | Steps | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Six Clerks** | Book 35, the children's rhyme | Use the rhyme's six verses to locate six tiny graves, each holding an Author's human belongings | The Clerk's Pen; unlocks the Colophon ending |
| 2 | **The Scraped Registrar** | Book 12, missing name | Track the erased Lord Registrar through wall carvings and reversed Verso records | Thale's private code |
| 3 | **The Year Everyone Forgot Tuesday** | Book 15 | Find Tuesday-named items and Tamsin's stone tray, and restore a lost day in the Verso | One extra day of Ink regen, and a small, odd feeling |
| 4 | **A Register of Lost Valleys** | Book 17 | Use SEEN at seven blank locations to reveal lost valley names | Sorrel can recite them, and epilogue line |
| 5 | **The Offset Sisters** | Book 85 | Reunite a woman in the Vale with the impression of her forgotten twin | Mirror Walk ability |
| 6 | **What Sank** | Book 8 | Dive through the Drowned Column's deepest levels to find the old capital's Ledger | Sea-map of hidden wrecks |
| 7 | **The Goat Debts** | Debt Pole of the Tosk Family | Follow a generational goat debt across three Quires until the interest becomes physically manifest | The Sum of All Goats |
| 8 | **Saint Slip's First Mistake** | Complete 3 others, then read the shrine at Saint Slip's Puddle | Trace the original misspelling back into the Verso | Saint Slip's First Mistake superboss |

## Standalone secrets and puzzles (40)

Vale:
1. The Standing Clock runs correctly only when nobody is looking.
2. A Goose Court precedent lets you sue a door.
3. Tithe Hill Stone shows a new line for co-op players.
4. Hidden stair beneath the Bench of the Nine Widows.
5. The Wrong Milestone points truly to a buried cache.
6. A complaint in the Gallows box asks for help, in your handwriting.
7. Saint Slip's Puddle returns a dropped coin as a slightly different coin.

Whitemarch:
8. A Frozen Wedding guest has a ring you can thaw and return.
9. The Empty Beacon lights only if WARM is used during a storm.
10. The Unfinished Letter continues in another cairn.
11. A goat at the Pole carries a tattooed map.
12. Blue Ice Cave contains a mammoth-sized misprint.
13. Sled Graveyard's oldest sled still runs.

Foxing Wood:
14. The Leaf That Does Not Fall falls if you apologise.
15. Oldest Stump contains 3,000 years of tiny carved complaints.
16. A hidden room in the Hollow Log Post Office.
17. Reading the Hanging Bell Tree's bells in order plays the main theme.
18. Antler Gate opens at dusk for those with a fox following them.
19. Mushroom Court verdict changes when ROTTEN is applied.

Saltmargin:
20. The Telescope, used at night, shows a different coast.
21. Whale Rib Arch sings in storms.
22. Anchor With No Rope is LIGHT, once.
23. A bottle contains a message addressed to the Witness.
24. Half a Lifeboat has its other half in the Verso.
25. Customs House records a shipment of "1 world, damaged."

Blotmere:
26. The Map Tree is accurate on one night per year.
27. Boat Full of Candles moves when DARK is written on it.
28. Heron Parliament will expel you for a good reason.
29. Reed Maze's solution is written on the player's own map.
30. Sunken Tax Office still accepts payments.
31. A lantern in the Graveyard belongs to Lamp Boy.

Cinder Folio:
32. A vent in the War Memorial speaks casualties aloud.
33. Obsidian Mirror shows the Second Edition before Q14.
34. Burned Treaty Stone can be read with WHOLE.
35. A very old Ash Fig tree still bears fruit.
36. Crater Overlook shows the Erasure's line at its true speed.

Erasure and Verso:
37. Writing LIT on The Chair makes it remember someone.
38. The Sign With One Word Left changes with each Stage.
39. River Lliuq can be crossed by walking backward.
40. Shrine of No One accepts an offering of Tamsin's Stone, and returns it without comment.

Find Godfrey Latch's best lock, which is locked.

Bring Ulf Crag a hot meal. Find Marit Sled's runner. Dig out the Toll Cairn. Listen to Hild Hale's story. Return Fenn Rook's furs.

Take a message to Fox Parliament. Bring Clover Rook chestnuts. Prune Willow Ames's patient. Light Elder Tamm's shrine. Find Ivy Halfmoon's arrow.

**Saltmargin (16):** Mend Hen Barnacle's net. Fetch Sea Holly. Help Pip Mizzen with knots. Salt Sally Brine's herring. Dive for Long John's anchor. Bring Grace Gunwale a plank. Deliver bait for Cuttle Ben. Smuggle one crate for Crab Hollis. Have Mother Tide read your fortune. Chase gulls from Wet Annie's. Find a message bottle. Repair a lobster pot. Return Dory Twice's oar.
