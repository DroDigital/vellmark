# Script: endings

## Final bosses

| Ending | Boss | Concept |
|---|---|---|
| Restoration | **The Cantor** | She does not want to hurt you. Her attacks soothe, slow, and put your words to sleep. Tamsin's Stone is unaffected |
| Blank | **Lord Registrar Thale** | He fights with records of everything you have done, summons your debts, and it is Thursday |
| Free Hand | **The Corrector** | The Ledger rewrites the arena's rules; your HUD gets corrected mid-fight |
| Colophon | **The Clerk** | You, unerased, who remembers everything and knows why not to do this |

## Boss lines

**The Cantor**

YSOLDE: I'm so sorry. Lie down.

YSOLDE: It's only a little further.

[On defeat:] YSOLDE: You'll read their birthdays now. All of you. Every one. Please be gentle with them.

**Lord Registrar Thale**

THALE: Eleven thousand, four hundred and six unpaid entries. Shall we begin?

THALE: I've always known what day this was.

[On defeat, he straightens his papers.] THALE: It was a very good sum. You could have - [He stops. He checks a page.] Yes. There it is.

**The Corrector**

TEXT: This office regrets the inconvenience.

TEXT: The Clerk has been found to be standing incorrectly.

TEXT: Please do not do that. Please do not do that. Please.

[On defeat:] TEXT: This office has not previously been asked to stop.

TEXT: Thank you.

**The Clerk**

THE CLERK: I did this so nobody could. You're about to do it anyway.

THE CLERK: You think you'll be different. So did Tom.

[On defeat:] THE CLERK: Then at least be sorry. It's traditional.

## Ending 1: The Restoration

**Final action:** the player draws the binding thread and signs off the Remainder.

[The pen moves. The list is scraped. Thread pulls tight through the canyon, with a sound like an enormous breath let out.]

**Outro (landscapes, no characters' faces):**

[Morning. The white on the horizon retreats, as a tide does. Fields come in, line by line, in crisp dark ink. It is the most orderly, beautiful view in the game.]

TEXT: The Ledger holds.

TEXT: Harvests are entered. Children are born, and read in, and their last days set down in good clear hand.

[The Remainder chosen by the player appears as a white patch on an otherwise perfect map. Then the map's border is redrawn so that it was never there.]

TEXT: It suffices for nine hundred Lines.

[A clerk in Ambry stamps a form. Edmund Tally is looking at an empty chair.]

EDMUND: Hm.

[He files the form.]

[The player's point of view: walking down a road in sunshine, carrying nothing. A dog runs up, wagging, then sniffs and walks on, as dogs do around strangers. In the inventory, a stone. The label reads as it did. Then, slowly, the stone's own entry disappears too.]

TEXT: ENTRY 4112.12.01.0001. A fine morning on Tithe Hill. Nobody is here.

TEXT: This is correct.

## Ending 2: The Blank

**Final action:** the player writes the line. The dialogue box slowly types it, one letter per button press. The player must complete every letter themselves.

TEXT: LET ALL PRIOR ENTRIES BE SCRAPED, AND THE WORK BEGIN AGAIN.

**Outro:**

[Evening. Every place in the game, briefly, with lanterns lit. There is no panic. No one notices anything is wrong. The letters begin to lift.]

[Goose Court adjourns. Provender Nell puts a pie on a table. Hob Corrigan signs an invoice, and it's blank when he passes it over. A goat walks across a field and is gone before it arrives.]

[Ysolde sits with her hands folded, by a window, and she closes her eyes.]

YSOLDE: There.

[Oake Farm. A woman sets a table. One place at a time, they are gone: the first, the second, the third.]

[She pauses with the fourth in her hand.]

MABEL: Oh.

MABEL: There you are.

[White.]

[A long time. No sound. Then a single point of ink in the middle of the screen.]

TEXT: In the beginning there was the Page.

TEXT: The Page was blank.

[A long hold. The next line does not appear.]

## Ending 3: The Free Hand

**Final action:** the player cuts the thread with the Penknife of Saint Slip. The world's pages come loose.

**Outro:**

[The Ledger falls apart, slowly, leaf by leaf, up into the sky over the white plain. The pages drift south. Each comes down somewhere. A farmer picks one up and uses it to wrap an onion.]

TEXT: There is no more Ledger.

[Words on the land begin to wander slightly. Road signs are wrong. A river takes a different course, and two villages begin an argument that will last for centuries.]

TEXT: Nothing is entered. Nothing is owed. Nobody knows.

[Ysolde, in the Blotmere. A child runs by her. She opens a blank book. She looks at it, for a long time. There is nothing to read about what happens to that child. She is shaking. She closes it.]

YSOLDE: Well.

[Thale, in the Hall of Record. He looks at his calendar. It is Friday.]

THALE: Hm.

[He laughs, for the only time in the game, and doesn't know what to do with his hands.]

[Oake Farm. Mabel sets out three places. She pauses and looks at the three places, frowning, without knowing why. She adds a fourth anyway, because she feels like it.]

[The player, by a roadside, at dusk. The inventory opens by itself. The stone: "Label: blank." A prompt appears, the first free-text entry in the game. Keyboard, or letter wheel.]

TEXT: Write a label.

[Whatever the player writes stays. The ink holds.]

[Then:]

TEXT: Name:

[The player types a name. For the first time, it stays.]

[The camera rises. The letters of the world are still there, slightly less straight than they were. In the far distance, in the white, something green starts to grow.]

TEXT: Ink's not dry.

## Ending 4: The Colophon (secret)

**Requires:** The Six Clerks chain and the Clerk's Pen. On any route, a new option appears on the last leaf. The player writes their own name above the final line.

**Outro:**

[The name stays. Light floods the room. The player's entry fills with amendments in a neat, familiar hand: first Name, then Title, then a long, flowing column of Additions.]

[A hall. Six very old, very small figures sit at desks, and look up. They look relieved.]

SAINT SLIP: Oh, thank goodness. Sorry. Sit there. It's not as bad as it looks.

[The desk. The Ledger is before you. Your hands are older. You can see the whole of Vellmark, in small print.]

TEXT: You may amend one entry.

[Choose: Tamsin Oake, Odile Marsh, or any erased person the player has recorded.]

[The chosen entry returns. Somewhere, a door opens. A girl walks into a kitchen, and a woman drops a plate.]

TEXT: There is no more room. Each new life requires an old one.

TEXT: Please select.

[The Ledger shows a list of the living, ordered by no principle at all. The cursor rests on the first name. There is no prompt to confirm or to cancel. The screen holds on this as long as the player keeps playing.]

TEXT: Take your time. You have all of it.

## Epilogue: "As Entered"

After each outro, the Ledger's hand writes a closing account. The Free Hand epilogue is in the player's own handwriting style, since the Ledger no longer exists. The Blank ending has none.

Build each line from flags. The order is: people, places, factions, then small things.

| Flag | Epilogue line |
|---|---|
| `tamsin_stone_kept` | "A stone was carried a long way." |
| `wick_alive` | "Wick the gravedigger was never paid." |
| `told_mabel` | "A woman in the Vale was told something she could not keep." |
| `ashby_readings >= 3` | "A funeral was held, several times, for someone nobody knew." |
| `ilse_names_added >= 5` | "At Last Ditto, the names were said." |
| `quillon_defected` | "Marshal Quillon resigned her commission, in writing, and it stayed written." |
| `anvil_army_held` | "The Cinder Folio's army was, for once, in the right place." |
| `clemence_lawsuit_won` | "Clemence Hart won her case against the Erasure. Damages remain unpaid." |
| `goats_settled` | "The Tosk family debt was settled in goats." |
| `killed_innocents >= 10` | "The Clerk has an outstanding balance. It was not forgiven." |

Add a line for each Quire's fate, each completed questline's outcome, and each follower's status. Keep it under 40 lines, and end with the ending's closing text.
