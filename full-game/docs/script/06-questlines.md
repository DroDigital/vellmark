# Script: major questlines

Each entry is a beat sheet. Writers expand it to full dialogue in the phase that builds each quest's Quire, as listed in `docs/SCOPE.md`, following `docs/bible/03-voice-and-dialects.md`.

## The Ledger Guard (7)

| ID | Title | Beats | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | Tallyman's Oath | Sergeant Dunstan Reeve swears you in, using a blank line. Collect one small debt from a widow. You may pay it yourself. | Guard rank |
| G2 | Bread Before Blades | Escort Emmeline Tarn's flour convoy to Tallymarket through hungry crowds. Violence loses reputation. | Rowan Pike's respect |
| G3 | The Toll That Was Abolished | Clear Old Tollway Fort. Its bandits are ex-Guard, abandoned without pay. Boss: The Toll. | Choice: hang or hire them |
| G4 | The Census Taker's Price | Evidence shows Sir Pym Vexley selling places on the census. Audit him. | Boss: Vexley, or expose him |
| G5 | Who Audits the Auditor | Discover that Osric Vane's records were altered from Thale's own office. | Quillon begins to doubt |
| G6 | Written and Witnessed | Hold Ambry against an Inkling tide while Edmund Tally evacuates the Lesser Ledger, one form at a time. | Edmund is saved or lost |
| G7 | The Marshal's Hand | Marshal Quillon asks you to decide what the Guard is for. Stand with Thale's list, or help her rewrite the oath. | Sets Guard's Q15 role |

## The Blank Choir (7)

Members can complete B1 to B6 without harming anyone.

| ID | Title | Beats | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | Clean Rooms | Serve a night with Sister Pale Anne, sitting with the dying. | Lay membership |
| B2 | Last Requests | Carry out three requests for dying patients, each stranger than the last. | Choir Whites |
| B3 | Bog Tea | Bring medicine to Mudlark during fever season. The Choir is simply helping. | Local gratitude |
| B4 | The Hymnal | Sister Evensong needs a missing verse, from the Sunken Chapel. It's a lullaby. | Silence ability |
| B5 | A Quiet Word | Sour's Zealots are guiding Redactors. Decide what Ysolde should be told. | Choir splits or holds |
| B6 | Lise | Lise's impression waits by River Lliuq in the Verso, and can be visited any time after Q8. If the player has met her, this quest changes completely, and Ysolde can be told. | Ysolde's trust |
| B7 | Let It Be Clean | The Choir walks together into the Erasure, singing. Walk with them, stop them, or stand aside. | Sets Choir's Q15 role |
