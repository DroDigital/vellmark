# Script: Act Two, "Verso"

Q9, Q10, and Q11 can be played in any order. Q12 unlocks after all three.

## Q8: The Back of the Page

**Location:** The Verso. **Boss:** Bailiff of the Verso. **Dungeon:** Reverse Penance.

[Step through the tear. Everything inverts: white ink, black paper, text mirrored. Sound runs slightly backward. Mirror Tithe Hill rises ahead.]

[A figure on the road tips its hat.]

OFFSET MAN: Goodbye! Thank you for leaving.

  > "What is this place?" -> OFFSET MAN: It's nothing, and nowhere, and I've never been happier to see you.

[Reading mode shows reversed text correctly. Spoken lines mean their opposite. Teach this through the Offset Man, who cheerfully says the opposite of everything.]

### Reverse Penance

[Through the mirror fields. Then: a village of twenty houses, drawn in white on black, every line reversed. It is Little Penance. It looks as if someone pressed the village against a window and took it away.]

[Figures move in the streets, repeating small actions. They are impressions, not people. A woman fills a lantern over and over.]

[At the end of the lane is a door, drawn with a child's care. There is a tray of pebbles on the step, with the labels written backward.]

TEXT: .yadseuT

[A girl sits on the step. She is thin white lines. She is sorting stones.]

TAMSIN: Olleh.

[Reading mode: Hello.]

TAMSIN: Did you find out what it is? What to put on the label?

  > "Not yet." -> TAMSIN: That's all right. There's no hurry here.
  > "They forgot you." -> TAMSIN: I know. It's quiet. Mum still does four, though. I can feel it from here.
  > "I remember you." -> TAMSIN: I know. That's why there's still some of me.
  > [Say nothing.] -> TAMSIN: You don't have to talk. I'm doing this one.

[She goes back to her stones. She is not in pain. She is not, in any useful sense, alive.]

The Bailiff of the Verso arrives to collect what is owed: anything impressed here that the page no longer needs. It is a towering figure of folded black paper. The player defends Tamsin's Door. After it falls, the path to the Library of Impressions can be seen, but its doors are closed for now.

## Q9: Let It Be Clean

**Location:** The Blotmere. **Boss:** Precentor Aldhelm Sour. **Dungeon:** The Choirhouse.

[Stilt Parish, lamps across black water. Lulu Mere tends the lamps, and agrees to take the player to the Choirhouse, without enthusiasm.]

LULU: They're good nurses, aren't they? That's the trouble, isn't it?

[The Choirhouse. Pale-robed members tend the dying in clean rooms. It is the kindest place the player has yet seen. Ysolde Wan washes a stranger's feet. She does not get up.]

YSOLDE: You've had a long road. Sit. Sister Anne will bring water.

  > "You worship the thing killing the world." -> YSOLDE: Yes.
  > "Why?" -> YSOLDE: Let me show you something.

[She takes the player into a small room. On a stand lies a page from a Lesser Ledger.]

YSOLDE: Lise Wan. Born 4089. I read it the day she was born. Mothers are told not to look.

TEXT: LISE WAN. DIES OF A FEVER IN HER TENTH YEAR, ON THE SECOND DAY OF HARROW.

YSOLDE: So I knew. For nine years, I knew. Every birthday, I knew which number we were on. I gave her very good birthdays.

YSOLDE: She died on the second of Harrow. Of a fever. There was nothing wrong with the book.

  > "I'm sorry." -> YSOLDE: Thank you. That's what people say.
  > "So you want everyone to forget?" -> YSOLDE: I want no child's last day written down before her first.
  > "The Erasure took a child I knew." -> YSOLDE: Then tell me: does her mother weep? [Player answers: "No."] YSOLDE: No. And you think I am monstrous for envying her.

YSOLDE: I am not asking you to agree. But you've seen it. You know it doesn't hurt.

[The player can tell her they have seen Lise's impression only after a later Verso visit. See Choir questline.]

Precentor Sour has been directing Zealots to help the Redactors find villages, against Ysolde's orders. He intercepts the player in the crypts below. His voice attacks silence Emendation in a spreading ring. Defeating him, exposing him, or rewriting him settles the Choir's internal split.

SOUR: She will wait a thousand Lines for it, and call that kindness. I have a schedule.

## Q10: Red Ink

**Location:** The Cinder Folio. **Dungeon:** The Rubric Forge. **Sees:** Caret flying toward the coast.

[Ashlar. Gudrun Anvil greets the player at volume.]

ANVIL: So you're the blank one! Sit down, you ruined hinge, and eat!

Only rubric ink, made at the Forge, can mark a Sealed word, and Odile needs it for the Scriptorium. Anvil will open the Forge in exchange for proving her army of 4,000 is not a rebellion against Scrivenhold. The player finds that it isn't. They are drilled to fight something that flies. She has told no one, as no one would have believed her.

ANVIL: War's not over till the other side stops. They've only changed their uniforms.

Tace Bellows, deep in the Forge, recognizes the tool marks on the player's hands.

TACE: Made a little scraper for the northern house, once. Best thing I ever made. Couldn't get it right, so I stopped when it was wrong enough. Whoever had it held it in a certain way. Here, like that.

[She adjusts your grip. It fits.]

TACE: Funny.

## Q11: Snow Blind

**Location:** The Whitemarch. **Dungeon:** The Frozen Quire.

[Hoarstead. Tor Hask hoards firewood, and refuses to discuss the north.]

HASK: Sent the census south last spring. Blank. Sent nothing since.

  > "Why?" -> HASK: Can't take what isn't counted. Maybe. Worth trying.

Bryn of the Cross guides the player to the Frozen Quire, a monastery sealed in ice, whose monks kept the road to the Last Scriptorium. Bryn carries a second bedroll and doesn't know why.

BRYN: Lost somebody. Don't know who. Hate them for it.

In the Quire, frozen monks sit at their desks, pens raised. The last living brother, Stig Lorn, has been writing the same sentence for four years, to keep himself from losing it.

STIG: The road north begins at the place where you stop being told where you are.

He gives the player the location of the Unwritten Road. The player also finds the Scriptorium's duty roster, with one line neatly removed.

TEXT: MASTER: O. MARSH. CLERK OF THE LAST PAGE: [struck].

## Q12: The Registrar's Arithmetic

**Location:** Debtors' Vault, Scrivenhold. **Boss:** Ser Anselm Gall-Hand. **Reveals:** the Ledger is full.

The player confronts Odile with the roster, and she refuses to answer.

ODILE: Not yet. I'm sorry. Ask me anything else, and I'll be very rude to you about it.

Marshal Quillon, troubled, asks the player to look at a sealed order. It leads to a vault beneath the Hall of Record, and to Thale's private accounts.

Thale's work, in the Vault: a wall of columns. Every birth entered. Every death scraped. A column showing the Ledger's remaining capacity, falling to zero 1,100 Lines ago. A list labelled **Remainder**: Quires and villages, ranked by their contribution to the realm. The Blotmere is first.

THALE: Please don't touch that. It's in order.

  > "You knew." -> THALE: Of course. It's arithmetic.
  > "You're choosing who gets erased." -> THALE: It is choosing. I am choosing the order.

THALE: The Ledger is full. It has been for a very long time. When your grandmother forgets her grandmother, that is the Ledger making room. That is what forgetting is. I thought you should hear it from someone who has done the sums.

THALE: There are children being born. They must be written somewhere. The dead ran out. Now it takes the living. If it is left alone, it takes them at random, and then it takes everything.

THALE: If I can feed it the least necessary tenth, I can bind the rest. I have seen the numbers. It holds.

  > "You can't decide that." -> THALE: Someone can. Would you prefer it be done by something that doesn't know their names?
  > "Who's on the list?" -> THALE: Everyone is on the list. I am on the list. I have read my entry. I die on a Thursday. I have a great deal to do.

Ser Anselm Gall-Hand, who has had his own past scraped so he cannot be swayed, blocks the way out.

ANSELM: I have no objection to you. I have no objections.

Quillon's choice depends on what the player reveals. If she sees the list, she leaves the Guard and becomes available as a follower.

## Q13: Reading Out

**Location:** Scrivenhold, Reading-Out quarter. **Reward:** Odile's Reading Glove. **Emotional peak of Act Two.**

[Odile's rooms at the College. She is packing. She has been expecting this for a while.]

ODILE: Thale put my name on the next page of his list. Not from spite. I know too much, and I take up a surprising amount of paper.

ODILE: I can't tell you who you were. If anybody writes it, or says it where it can be set down, the Ledger will have your direction. All I can tell you is where it's kept: in the Library of Impressions. You'll need my glove to open it.

  > "Run." -> ODILE: With this hip?
  > "I won't let this happen." -> ODILE: That's kind, and it isn't up to you.
  > "Were you my teacher?" -> ODILE: You were my worst student. You kept asking why.

[She reaches out and reads your face with her fingers, carefully, as she did your hand in Ambry.]

ODILE: Still nothing there. Well done.

[Later. The player is crossing the College court. Above Reading-Out, a short, level black line draws itself in the sky. It's over in a moment.]

[Odile's rooms are a storeroom. Her door has a different name on it. Provost Sable walks by.]

SABLE: Can I help you?

  > "Where is Odile?" -> SABLE: Who?
  > "Odile Marsh. Your colleague for forty years." -> SABLE: I'm afraid I don't know the name. Are you quite well? [Pause.] Forgive me, I seem to have - I came down here for something. How odd.

[On the storeroom shelf, a single reading glove. Only the player can see that it matters.]

### The funeral

Father Lamb Ashby, in the Reading-Out quarter, conducts funerals for anyone who asks. He has started leaving a slot open.

ASHBY: You want a reading-out. For whom?

[The name does not fade in the player's mouth. Only their own does.]

  > "Odile Marsh." -> ASHBY: Friend of yours?

ASHBY: I don't know her. No one will. But the form says someone must read the entry aloud, and the form doesn't say the entry needs to exist. Tell me about her, and I'll say it back.

[The player is presented with statements, and chooses as many as they like.]

  > "She was blind and read everything anyway."
  > "She was very rude to me."
  > "She wrote down what I told her, even when the ink wouldn't stay."
  > "She knew all along."
  > "It's the habit that counts."

[Ashby repeats each sentence, formally. At the end:]

ASHBY: Paid up. Written and witnessed.

ASHBY: Now. Who was she?

[He has already forgotten. The player may tell him again. He will read it again. There is no limit on this interaction, and no reward.]

## Q14: Offset

**Location:** The Verso, Library of Impressions. **Boss:** The Second Edition. **Reward:** Penknife of Saint Slip. **Ends Act Two. Triggers Erasure Stage 2.**

[With Odile's glove, the Library's doors open. Inside are endless shelves of reversed pages: every entry ever scraped. The player searches by touch, in darkness. Odile's voice appears only as her handwriting on the walls, fading.]

TEXT: You'll want the last shelf. It's always the last shelf. Do try to stand up straight.

[A single sheet. Reversed. Reading mode resolves it line by line.]

TEXT: ENTRY. THE CLERK OF THE LAST PAGE.

TEXT: Instruction received from the Corrector, Line 4,111. To be entered in the Clerk's hand: LET ALL PRIOR ENTRIES BE SCRAPED, AND THE WORK BEGIN AGAIN.

TEXT: Clerk's reply: Why.

TEXT: Corrector's reply: There is no more room.

TEXT: Clerk's reply: Then let there be no more Clerk.

[Lines beneath it, in another hand: Odile's.]

TEXT: You stubborn, stupid child. You've taken the only pen in the world and thrown yourself in the river with it. I shall go south. I will find you and I will tell you nothing. I am so proud I could be sick.

[The player has a choice of responses, which no one hears.]

  > "I did this to myself."
  > "I chose this."
  > "I wish I remembered choosing it."

[The page begins to change, correcting itself. From the far shelves comes a figure, in the player's exact shape, wearing clean clothes. Every letter on it is perfect.]

THE SECOND EDITION: A fair copy has been prepared. You may stand down.

THE SECOND EDITION: I have your name. It's a very nice name. Would you like it?

  > "Yes." -> THE SECOND EDITION: Then stand still. [Fight begins anyway, on the Corrector's standing instructions.]
  > "No." -> THE SECOND EDITION: Noted. [Fight begins.]

It fights using the player's own words and last weapon type. When it falls, the penknife remains: small, crooked, and badly made.

TEXT: PENKNIFE OF SAINT SLIP. This was a mistake. Sorry.

[Return to the world through a new tear in Scrivenhold. The Gutters district is pale at the edges. Across the Vale, the white horizon has come closer.]
