# Script: Author quests and side quests

## Author quests (6)

Each Author communicates only through marginalia written on nearby objects. After Q17, each quest gains a final, bitter note from the clerk the Author once was.

| ID | Author | Title | Beats | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | The Rubricator | Paid in Full | Settle three blood-debts at Ashlar. Each resolution makes another. Marginalia: PAID. NOT PAID. PAID IN FULL BY OTHERS. | Red Verdict |
| A2 | Mother Vellum | All of It | Feed the Eelmother's starving young and an orphan flock at the Shrine, using your own Health. | The Last Warm Hide |
| A3 | The Compositor | Align This | Straighten the Crooked Boardwalk, the Wrong Milestone, and the Lost Road Marker, each of which resists. | Justified Margin |
| A4 | Gall | A While Longer | Find the last person who remembers a long-dead child, and sit with them to the end. | The Black Oak |
| A5 | The Gilder | The Old Price | Accept a sequence of wonderful bargains, each slightly worse. You can walk away at any time, and that's the only win. | Fair Market Value |
| A6 | Saint Slip | Sory | Clean up after a chain of the Saint's mistakes, each caused by your fixing the last. | Second Thought |

## Rewriting characters

Using NAMELESS or LOYAL on a listed character succeeds, and it should feel like a violation.

| Character | LOYAL | NAMELESS |
|---|---|---|
| Thale | Serves you with perfect, hollow precision; stops doing sums | Ledger cannot find his Thursday; he panics |
| Ysolde | Abandons the Choir and cannot say why she cries | Forgets Lise, and is happy. The player has to see this |
| Sable | Says what you want, and becomes useless as a scholar | Publishes under no name, and is finally honest |
| Quillon | Follows any order | Leaves the city and is never found |
| Pell | Stops stealing | One of her three names remains, and it is the wrong one |
| Sour | Turns on the Choir | Silent forever |
| Ellery | Works until she collapses | City's records stop recognizing her authority |
| Twice | Pays her taxes | Nobody fears her; wrecked at her own harbour |
| Hask | Sends firewood south, and his people freeze | His census is finally blank, and it works |
| Rue | Forgives everyone at once, horrifyingly | Forgets the grudge, and with it her entire life |
| Lull | Draws an accurate map | The Blotmere cannot find him |
| Anvil | Disbands her army | Her army no longer knows who to report to |
