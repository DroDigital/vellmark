### Textures

Use as material maps (albedo, normal, and roughness) and as input to the bake for per-cell glyph data: [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com) and [Poly Haven textures](https://polyhaven.com/textures). Both are CC0.

### VFX

All in-game effects are made of letters. [Kenney Particle Pack](https://kenney.nl/assets/particle-pack) (80 CC0 sprites) may be used only as hidden masks that drive where letters spawn and how they fade. Write everything else as procedural letter emitters.

### Music

Do not use pre-recorded music packs. Compose the score as code and data, played through CC0 samples, so that the soundtrack is cohesive, original, and safe from content-ID claims.

- [Versilian Community Sample Library](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL) (CC0)
- [VSCO 2 Community Edition](https://versilian-studios.com/vsco-community/) (CC0, using only the official Vanilla SFZ or Raw WAV versions)

Inventory the libraries when importing. Synthesize whatever is missing, including hurdy-gurdy drones and the formant choir.

### Fonts

The renderer uses two typefaces and only their 95 printable ASCII characters:

- **Small letters:** [Tiny5](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/tiny5) (OFL), a 5-pixel font, for cells drawn at about 6 to 16 px. It stays legible at a few pixels.
- **Large letters:** [IM Fell English](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/imfellenglish) (OFL), based on historic English printing types, for close-up text and books.

### Shader references

Port the techniques to WGSL, and keep each project's notice:

- [UnrealEngineSkyAtmosphere](https://github.com/sebh/UnrealEngineSkyAtmosphere) (MIT) for sky and atmosphere
- [XeGTAO](https://github.com/GameTechDev/XeGTAO) (MIT, archived) for ambient occlusion, without its TAA path
