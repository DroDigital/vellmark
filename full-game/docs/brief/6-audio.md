# Section 6: audio

## 6. Audio (approved CC0 recordings and samples, plus synthesis)

- Layered wind, water, fire, footsteps per surface, creaking timber, and birdsong, combining approved recordings with procedural variation so sounds never repeat identically.
- The sonic signature is writing: pen scratches when Inscriptions change, typewriter and printing-press impacts for combat and heavy events, and pages turning.
- **Every word has a motif.** Each of the 35 words has its own phrase of three to five notes in the main theme's mode, held on the word's sheet and played by synthesis whenever the word is written. A player should come to know a word by ear.
  - Charging holds the first note and lets it rise. Writing with both hands doubles the phrase an octave apart.
  - A typo plays its word's motif with one wrong note.
  - A sort changes the delivery: Exclaim accents it, Comma echoes it once, Ellipsis plays it late, and Query leaves it unresolved.
  - A compound plays both motifs together and resolves them into one chord. The 30 pairs must each sound consonant.
  - An annotated enemy hums its word's motif faintly, and a word on a weapon colours each hit with its first note.
  - A Standing Page plays its word's motif across the whole valley when the word is learned. The main theme is built from the motifs of the Vale's four words, so the player has been hearing them since the title.
  - Motifs count as important sounds and are captioned by word. No two may share the same intervals. They use synthesis and approved samples only, and never voice credits.
- Generative modal folk score, sequenced in code and performed with approved CC0 instrument samples: bowed strings, plucked instruments, hurdy-gurdy-like drones, and formant-synthesized choir, with one strong main theme that recurs across exploration, combat, and the ending.
- Voice uses 10 AI base voices, processed by the game into distinct characters as defined in `docs/voice/cast-and-budget.md`. The credit limit is strict.
- 3D spatialization, reverb by environment, and music that responds to discovery and danger.
