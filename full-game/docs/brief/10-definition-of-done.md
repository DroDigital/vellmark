# Section 10: definition of done

## 10. Definition of done

### Every release, including Release 1

- A new player can install it through Steam or itch.io on Windows, macOS, Linux, or Steam Deck, start a new game, and finish everything that release contains with no developer tools.
- Two people can play all of it together, joining and leaving at any time, using any mix of controllers and keyboard and mouse.
- A save from every earlier released build still loads, and no reachable state can make the story unwinnable.
- Every closed road has a written reason in the world.
- The credits, `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt`, and `docs/AI_DISCLOSURE.md` are complete, and the license audit passes on the final builds.
- Every gate has saved evidence in `docs/evidence/`.
- Everything in `docs/SCOPE.md` marked for that release is shipped, complete, and validated. Do not quietly reduce a target or change a tier. If one proves unrealistic, tell me and let me choose.
- A stranger looking at a landscape screenshot would take it for a handsome, realistic RPG. The same stranger shown a close-up, or close reading, would ask, "What is that game?"
- A player who stops to look at a wall finds something worth reading.
- A player asked what they did in their first hour describes something they rewrote.
- A player who has played any action RPG needs no explanation of the combat, the loot, or how to cast, and fights with a weapon in one hand and a word in the other without being told to.
- Someone who has never heard of the game can play its first hour with no help and no invented term explained to them.

### 1.0

- A new player can finish the main quest.
- All four endings, the intro, and every outro can be reached and watched in a normal playthrough.
- The whole realm and every 1.0 target in Sections 4 and 5 are shipped.
- No Reserve entry is referenced anywhere in the game.
