# Section 1.1: license rules, manifest, AI gate

## 1.1 Approved assets and libraries

Third-party assets save work on geometry, motion, and sound. They are raw material, and the glyph renderer gives them Vellmark's look. No third-party texture, sprite, or UI element is ever shown directly, so ASCII purity still holds.

### License rules

- **Art, audio, and sample content:** CC0 only, except for the single optional source marked below and AI-generated content that passes the AI gate.
- **Code and shaders:** MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD, Zlib, ISC, BSL-1.0, Unicode-3.0, CC0-1.0, or MPL-2.0. Generate `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt` from the dependency tree, include statically linked code such as SDL3 and DXC, and show the required notices in the in-game credits.
- **Fonts:** SIL Open Font License. Ship the license text with the game.
- **Banned:** anything with NC, ND, SA, or GPL terms, or with unclear terms such as "no copyright" without a real license. Also banned: AI-generated content that has not passed the AI gate, ripped game assets, BBC Sound Effects, Mixkit music, Mixamo, and Shadertoy code unless that specific shader states a permissive license.
- Every license is per item on mixed sites. On OpenGameArt or itch.io, check the individual asset page and accept only a written CC0 grant. Pack contents must match their stated license.
- Do not use any creator's logo or imply endorsement. Do not resell or redistribute the source packs as standalone files.
- Do not scrape websites. Poly Haven requires permission for scraping and has separate API terms, so download individual assets or follow those terms.

### Asset manifest

Every third-party file is recorded in `third_party/manifest.ron` with source URL, author, license, date checked, and SHA-256. The bake fails on any unlisted or changed file.

Many sources need a browser click, payment-optional checkout, or an account. If a download cannot be automated, do not work around it. Write `third_party/DOWNLOADS.md` listing exactly what I need to fetch, and continue with other work until the files appear in `third_party/inbox/`. Keep the repository private. Store converted source files in Git LFS so CI can bake them, and ask me before making any part of the repository public, because source packs and Sonniss sounds must not be redistributed as standalone files.

Use free tiers first. Quaternius's Standard tiers contain roughly 60 to 70 percent of each larger pack. If a paid tier would add something that is truly needed, list it for my decision.

### AI-generated assets: the AI gate

Free AI-generated models, textures, sounds, and images are accepted only if both quality and legality are confirmed. Use them where they fill a real gap or clearly beat the approved CC0 option. Everything starts in `third_party/quarantine/` and cannot reach a pack file until it passes every check below and I approve it.

**Legality checks.** Record all of these in `third_party/ai_provenance.ron`, and archive a dated copy of each license and terms page:

1. **Tool license.** The exact model version's license for both code and weights must allow commercial use, in Germany and the EU and in worldwide distribution, and must allow use of its outputs. Check the license at its source. Do not rely on summaries or on a project's headline license alone.
2. **Provenance.** Record the tool, version, seed, prompts, settings, and date. Prompts must not name artists, studios, franchises, or characters. Every input image or audio file must itself be approved.
3. **Pre-made free AI packs.** Accept one only if it has a written license from the creator, names the generator and version, and meets check 1. Otherwise reject it. A "CC0" or "no copyright" label on its own does not confirm legality.
4. **Originality.** Review each asset for resemblance to existing characters, logos, brands, artworks, or recordings. Run reverse-image or 3D-reference checks for visual assets and an audio-fingerprint check for sounds and music where possible. Reject anything with watermarks, signatures, or a recognizable source.
5. **Ownership limit.** Fully AI-generated output may have little or no copyright protection, and its status under German law is unsettled. Do not use unmodified AI output for anything Vellmark must own: the logo, key art, main theme, named heroes and bosses, or store page art.
6. **My sign-off.** Give me a short review sheet for each batch, with previews and the checks above. Only I can set `approved_by_owner: true`. If any check is uncertain, the asset is rejected.

**Quality checks.** An AI asset must meet the same bar as everything else:

- **Models:** clean topology, sensible triangle count, no melted or fused parts, correct scale, clean UVs where used, and a readable silhouette from every angle. Rigged models must deform correctly.
- **Textures:** no warped details, repeating artifacts, or baked-in lighting.
- **Audio:** no metallic artifacts, smearing, or broken tails, and it must hold up on headphones.
- **All assets:** fit Vellmark's art direction after going through the renderer, and pass the variety report. Show side-by-side captures against the best CC0 alternative.

**Disclosure.** Maintain `docs/AI_DISCLOSURE.md`, listing every use of generative AI that reaches players. This includes assets and all text written with AI help, such as quests, dialogue, books, and item descriptions. Use it to fill out the Pre-Generated AI section of Steam's content survey in detail, and apply the correct AI tag on itch.io. Never use a "No AI" label. Vellmark uses no AI generation while the game is running.
